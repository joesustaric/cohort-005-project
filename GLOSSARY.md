# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and instructors build them.

## Purchases and refunds

**Purchase**:
A learner's one-off payment for a single course, which grants them access to it.
_Avoid_: Subscription, order, enrollment

**Refund request**:
A learner asking for the full refundable amount of a Purchase back. If it is approved, the Purchase no longer grants access to the course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, reversal, chargeback

**Refund window**:
The purchase day plus the following 14 calendar days, counted in whole days with the last day included.

**Refundable amount**:
The amount the learner actually paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Course price, list price

**Refund eligibility**:
A Purchase is eligible for a refund when all of these are true: it is within its Refund window, no Refund request is already open on it, it is not a claimed team seat, the learner has watched under 2 hours of the course, and the learner has completed under half of its lessons.

**Approved** / **Declined**:
The two possible outcomes of a Refund request. An approved request returns the Refundable amount and ends access to the course.
_Avoid_: Rejected, reversed, completed

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute raised by the learner's card issuer rather than with us. It is not the same thing as a Refund request.
